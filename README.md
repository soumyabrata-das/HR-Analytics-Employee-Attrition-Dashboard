# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Human Resources (HR) data to identify employee attrition patterns, understand workforce demographics, and uncover factors associated with employee turnover.

Using Microsoft Excel for data management and Microsoft Power BI for data visualisation, I developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard that transforms raw employee data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps HR professionals and business stakeholders understand workforce composition, monitor employee attrition, and identify potential areas for improvement in employee retention.

## Business Objective

The primary objective is to analyse employee attrition and identify workforce trends across different organisational dimensions, including:

- Employee demographics and age distribution.
- Department-wise and job-role-wise attrition.
- Salary distribution and compensation trends.
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Overtime patterns and their relationship with attrition.
- Employee experience and tenure.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **1,470 employee records and 38 attributes**.

Key data attributes include:

| Category | Attributes |
|---|---|
| Employee Demographics | Age, Gender, Marital Status, Education |
| Employment Details | Department, Job Role, Job Level |
| Compensation | Monthly Income, Salary Slab, Percent Salary Hike |
| Employee Satisfaction | Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance |
| Work Experience | Total Working Years, Years at Company, Years in Current Role |
| Attrition | Attrition Status, Attrition Count |
| Work Conditions | Business Travel, Overtime, Distance from Home |

## Key Findings

Based on the dataset analysis:

- **Overall Attrition:** 237 employees have an attrition status of Yes, representing approximately 16.12% of the workforce.
- **Age Group:** Employees aged 18–25 demonstrate the highest attrition rate among the age groups, at approximately 35.8%.
- **Overtime:** Employees working overtime have a higher attrition rate (30.5%) compared with employees not working overtime (10.4%).
- **Job Role:** Sales Representatives demonstrate a comparatively high attrition rate of approximately 39.8%.
- **Salary Slab:** Employees earning up to 5k have an attrition rate of approximately 21.8%.
- **Marital Status:** Single employees demonstrate a higher attrition rate (25.5%) compared with married employees (12.5%).

These findings highlight workforce segments that may benefit from further investigation into employee engagement, compensation, workload, and retention strategies.

*Note: These are descriptive findings and do not establish causation.*
